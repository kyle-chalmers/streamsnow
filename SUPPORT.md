# Getting help

StreamSnow has one maintainer and a set of automated helpers, so where you ask decides how
fast you get an answer.

| You want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| Ask how to do something | [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/kyle-chalmers/streamsnow/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| Suggest an idea or talk through a change | [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/kyle-chalmers/streamsnow/discussions/categories/ideas) |
| Show what you built | [Discussions → Show and tell](https://github.com/kyle-chalmers/streamsnow/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) |
| Report a bug, or request a well-scoped feature | [New issue](https://github.com/kyle-chalmers/streamsnow/issues/new/choose) (pick a form) |
| Report a vulnerability | Privately, per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never in a public issue |
| Fix a first-run or deploy error | [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md) first: most errors have a numbered entry |

## What to expect

- **Issues are triaged automatically** within minutes: labeled by type and area, checked
  for duplicates, and, if details are missing, sent back with a list of what to add. Issues
  waiting on information close after 14 quiet days; you can always reopen.
- **The maintainer reviews triaged issues** and either approves them for implementation,
  asks a question, or explains why they are out of scope. Expect this within about a week.
- **Approved issues are often implemented by an AI agent** and reviewed by the maintainer
  before merge. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#how-issues-move).
- Ideas in Discussions that gain support and fit the [mission](README.md#mission) are turned
  into issues by the maintainer.

Please include the output of `streamsnow --version` and how you installed StreamSnow in
anything you report, and never paste credentials, Snowflake account identifiers or real data.
